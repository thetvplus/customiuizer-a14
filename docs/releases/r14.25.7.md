## 简体中文

自 r14.25.5 起的主要更新：

- **动态岛动效**：重新打磨页面吸入、岛体回弹和左右揉动，连续切换更连贯。
- **活动与消息**：音乐、通话、闹钟、计时等持续活动统一短岛，消息按正文呈现长短岛；上滑一次退出，也可保留静止小岛，支持横屏布局。
- **音乐体验**：改善波形、切歌封面和加载时的显示，减少重复会话引起的闪动；小窗排除可按应用设置。
- **电量提示**：新增可选的充电与低电量反馈。
- **文件夹图标模糊**：新增独立开关，默认关闭，修改后需重启桌面；MIUI / POCO 的实际效果取决于 ROM 与系统模糊条件。
- **Dock 与 Duo**：调整材质、尺寸和对齐，修复斜滑误触及壁纸切换后的显示问题。
- **设置与日常修复**：整理动态岛设置，改善搜索、说明与数值输入；修正日期格式、应用锁、音量键、输入法收回及桌面标题、组件显示等问题。

[完整更新记录](https://github.com/thetvplus/customiuizer-a14/blob/r14.25.7/CHANGELOG.md)

## English

Highlights since r14.25.5:

- **Island motion**: refined page absorption, island recoil and kneading, with more continuous handoffs during rapid switching.
- **Activities and messages**: music, calls, alarms and timers use the short island; messages choose their form by content. Swipe up once to dismiss, optionally keep a still idle island, and use the island in landscape.
- **Music**: improved waveforms, artwork and loading states, with less flicker from mirrored sessions. Floating-app exclusions can be selected per app.
- **Battery feedback**: optional charging and low-battery indicators.
- **Folder-icon blur**: a separate switch, off by default. Restart the Launcher after changing it; MIUI / POCO appearance depends on the ROM and system blur conditions.
- **Dock and Duo**: refined materials, sizing and alignment; fixed accidental diagonal-swipe actions and wallpaper-change display issues.
- **Settings and everyday fixes**: clearer Island settings, search, descriptions and number entry, plus fixes for date formats, app lock, volume keys, IME dismissal, Launcher titles and widgets.
